## 1. First Two Weeks (Days 1–14)

### HR & Benefits
- Submit **W-4**, **I-9**, direct-deposit info  
- Enroll in Benefits Connect

### Setup OneNote for Onboarding Notes and Personalized Onboarding Plan

The generic onboarding plan should be copied into a new OneNote for the new hire. Then it should be personalized with the new hire's name and any other relevant information. The manager and new hire should work together to fill in the onboarding plan with specific tasks and goals for the new hire's first 30, 60, and 90 days. The new hire should also be encouraged to add their own notes and questions as they go through the onboarding process.
- Create a new section for each week of onboarding
- put 30, 60 and 90 day checkpoints in the outlook calendar inviting the manager and new hire

### Foundational Training
- Security awareness & compliance  
- Confirm access: Confluence, Jira, GitLab, TFE  
- Cloud-provider fundamentals
- Internal platform/tool deep-dive

### Team & Process Orientation
- Review team purpose and roles  
- Sprint process walkthrough (ceremonies, backlog, release)  
- Intro to Architecture Review Board & meeting cadence  
- Short training on cloud & datacenter strategy
