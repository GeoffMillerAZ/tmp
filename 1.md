1. Centralized Governance & Security
	1.	Organization Resource Policies
	•	Enforce org-level guardrails (e.g., SCPs, tagging policies) to maintain consistent security, compliance, and operational rules. We already do SCPs but they should be re-evaluated.
	1.	DNS Firewall
	•	Leverage Route 53 DNS Firewall for consistent threat protection across accounts.


1. Unified Network Architecture
	1.	Centralized North/South Inspection VPC
	•	A single entry/exit point per region, funneling traffic through a central inspection layer and optionally a central API Gateway.
	1.	Determine East/West Inspection
	•	Evaluate whether traffic between regions/accounts also goes through a shared inspection path or if it’s region-specific.
	1.	Centralized Ingress Pattern via API Gateway
	•	Provide a single interface for service discovery and routing.
	•	If needed, set up private endpoints for external providers.
	1.	Transit Gateway for Aggregation & Network Segmentation
	•	Simplify cross-account and cross-region connectivity with a hub-and-spoke model.
	•   This will optimize centralized network services and management and reduce complexity.
	1.	Explore VPC Lattice
	•	Potentially unify service-to-service communication within and across accounts.
	•   Potentially useful for services that will remain in the datacenter or another cloud but need to speak to AWS. Or for services that are migrating to AWS but are not yet fully migrated.

1. Shared Services & Resource Management
	
	1.	Utilize Resource Access Manager (RAM)
	•	Share commonly used resources (e.g., shared VPC subnets, Route 53 zones, etc.) across accounts.
	•	Reduces the massive VPC sprawl and cost headaches while giving api encapsulation on a per team and per environment basis.
	1.	EventBridge Aggregation for Shared Services
	•	Aggregate events from multiple accounts to allow for event-driven architectures.

1. Automation & Cost Management
	1.	Environment Claim Automation Service
	•	This service is already developed but needs to be adapted for new platform.
	•	Automatically spin down non-critical resources when idle.
	•	Huge cost-saving potential and improves operational efficiency.
	1.	Event-Driven Platform for Backups & Lifecycle Management
	•	Automate snapshot creation, retention, and cleanup.
	1.	Further Cost-Saving Automations
	•	Use event-driven logic to identify underutilized or orphaned resources.
	•	If tag compliance is enforced, this can be done automatically.

1. Architectural & Organizational Considerations
	1.	Shared VPC Strategy
	•	Decide on segmentation dimensions (Public vs. Non-Public vs. Compliance vs. Business Unit) for creating shared VPCs across accounts.

1. Tag Compliance
	1.	Automated Tag Enforcement & Security Setup
	•	Enforce mandatory tags (e.g., environment, business unit, compliance domain).
	•	Prevent resource creation if tags are missing or invalid.
	1.	“Permit to Operate” Tag Validation
	•	Block resources that do not meet certain tag or compliance criteria.

1. Tagging & Observability
	1.	Event-Driven Platform for Tagging
	•	Auto-tag indirectly managed resources upon creation (e.g., EBS volumes, snapshots).
	1.	Enhanced Monitoring & Audit Trails
	•	Combine tags with a centralized logging/metrics platform for improved observability and cost reporting.
